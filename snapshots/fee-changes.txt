Citizenship fees: If you didn’t pay or only partially paid
If you don’t pay the full fee, or only pay part of it, we’ll return your application.
We’ll process your application only
after you pay the full amount, and
after you include the receipt with your resubmitted application
If you mail your application, the applicable fee is based on the date you sign your application, even if there’s a delay before we receive it.
Permanent residence fees: If you paid the old fees with your application
When you apply online, we receive your application immediately. Before you submit it, check that you paid the correct amount.
If you mail your application, there will be a delay between when you send it and when we receive it. The fees may change while it’s in the mail. In most cases, we won’t reject your application if
you paid the old fee
the application was complete, and
you mailed it before the fee change
In this case, we’ll tell you how to pay the difference between the new and old fees.
If you waited to pay your right of permanent residence fee
If you applied without paying the right of permanent residence fee, you must pay the new amount. This applies even if you already paid the processing fee and applied before the fee change.
How to pay the fee difference
If you paid the old fee for your application, we’ll ask you to pay the difference and tell you how to submit your receipt.
To get your receipt:
Calculate the fee difference
You must pay the difference between the old fee and the new fee.
You must pay the difference for each fee that changed, including those for family members.
Make the payment
Go to the Make an additional payment or pay other fees category in our online payment tool.
Enter the amount
Under “Quantity”, enter the total fee difference.
You’ll get 1 receipt for each payment. You can pay all fees in 1 transaction or repeat this step for multiple receipts.
You must create an account or sign in to finish paying.
Submit your receipt
Follow the instructions in the request we sent you.
